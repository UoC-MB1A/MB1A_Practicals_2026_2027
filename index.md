The aim of this series of practical sessions is to develop the skills to independently perform biological and mathematical analyses using the programming language R. There will be eight two hour practicals, four in Michaelmas term and four in Lent term.

::: {.callout-tip .partmenu}

### Sessions

Across the eight practicals we will learn how to use R for analyses related to the following topics:

- Practical 1: **Introduction to R and RStudio**
- Practical 2: **Data frames and plotting**
- Practical 3: **Probability**
- Practical 4: **Statistics**
- Practical 5: **Bioinformatics**
- Practical 6: **Further statistics**
- Practical 7: **Mathematical modelling - systems of differential equations**
- Practical 8: **Mathematical modelling - modelling epidemics**

:::


## Levels

Materials and exercises are labelled as follows:

| Icon | Level | Description |
|-|-|----------|
| [{{< fa solid circle >}}]{.core-text} | [**Core**]{.core-text} | Essential material that everyone should read and complete, unless you are already very comfortable with the topic. |
| [{{< fa solid flask >}}]{.rec-text}  | [**Recommended**]{.rec-text} | Everyone should read and understand this material, and ideally do the exercises, but if you're running short of time you can move on without completing the exercises yourself.|
| [{{< fa solid magnifying-glass >}}]{.extension-text}  | [**Extension**]{.extension-text} | Provides either extra depth on a topic or additional skills which are not essential for other practicals. Exercises will have a little less guidance than the core and recommended exercises. |
| [{{< fa solid rocket >}}]{.bonus-text}  | [**Bonus**]{.bonus-text} | Extra challenges just for fun, for people who are working quickly or are very comfortable with the topic. Exercises will have much less guidance than the other levels. |



## Exams and assessment

The material in these practicals is **not in itself examinable**. Each practical is recorded as either completed or not completed.

::: {.callout-important #imp-completed}

### Completion

At the end of each practical, to be marked as having **completed** the practical, please show a demonstrator the main Quarto file you have been working on.

:::

Some of the material does overlap with or build upon material from the lectures, and in these cases you **may be examined** on the lecture material.
